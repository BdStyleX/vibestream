## What we collect
- **Account data:** email address, display name, and a unique user ID.
- **Content you post:** captions, photos, videos, poll questions and your votes, and likes.
- **Safety data:** reports you submit and the list of users you block.
- **Basic technical data** processed by our service providers (Google Firebase) such as IP address and crash or connection logs.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not show third-party ads.

## How we use it
- To create and run your account and show your posts and interactions.
- To keep the community safe (reviewing reports, removing harmful content, preventing abuse).
- To fix problems and keep the service running.

## Who processes data
We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage) to store and process data. Posts you publish are visible to other signed-in VibeStream users.

## Retention and deletion
You can delete your account inside the app (Profile > Delete my account). This removes your login, profile and posts. Backups and logs may take a short time to clear.
If you cannot access the app, email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from your registered address with the subject "Delete my account" and we will delete it.

## User-generated content
You must not post illegal, hateful, harassing, sexually explicit or violent content. You can report posts and block users. We may remove content and suspend accounts that break these rules.
